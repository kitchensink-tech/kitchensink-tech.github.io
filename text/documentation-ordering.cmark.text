The list of pages and the previous and next links of a [documentation page](/documentation-layout.html ) follow the order of the pages that use the `documentation` layout, and only those.

# The order field

The optional `order` number of the `base:build-info.json` section gives the position of a page: pages with a smaller number come first.

# Pages without an order

Pages without an `order` come after the numbered ones, sorted by title. A documentation where no page has an `order` is thus sorted by title.

# Groups

The optional `group` of the `base:build-info.json` section is the name of the group a page is listed under. The pages of a group are always listed together: a group stands where its first page does, and its other pages follow in order. Pages without a `group` form a group of their own, listed without a name.

# Publication status

The order only decides where a page stands among the others. A page follows the publication status rules of any other layout.
